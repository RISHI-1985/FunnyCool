Is Crackstone Really Gone?

The fiery demise of Joseph Crackstone seemed pretty final, but is any villain ever truly gone in a world of magic and necromancy? Some fans believe that a piece of his soul may have latched onto a new host (perhaps Tyler?) or that his followers might find a way to resurrect him. His powerful magic and deep-seated hatred for outcasts make him too good a villain to use for just one season.

The Identity of Wednesday's Stalker

The first season ended with a major cliffhanger: an unknown stalker sending Wednesday threatening texts and photos. Who could it be? Theories range from a resurrected Laurel Gates to a new, unseen character with a grudge. Some even speculate it could be a member of a rival outcast faction, like the vampires or a different sect of sirens, aiming to challenge the established order at Nevermore.

The True Nature of the Addams Family

While the show focuses on Wednesday, there are hints that the Addams family as a whole has a much larger and more mysterious role in the world of outcasts. Could they be the secret guardians of some ancient power? Is Gomez's past more complicated than just a simple murder accusation? Season 2 could delve deeper into the family's history, revealing their true influence and the source of their unique abilities.
