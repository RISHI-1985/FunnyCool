Latest Buzz

Season 2 Wraps Filming in Romania

August 15, 2025

The cast and crew have officially wrapped filming for the second season in the beautiful, gothic landscapes of Romania. Sources say the new setting adds an even darker and more authentic feel to the show. The production team shared a wrap photo on social media, thanking the local crews for their hospitality.

Source: Variety

Casting News

Steve Buscemi Joins the Cast

July 28, 2025

In a move that has fans buzzing, veteran actor Steve Buscemi has been confirmed to join the cast for Season 2. While his role is being kept under wraps, insiders speculate he will be playing a new, eccentric teacher at Nevermore Academy. His unique style is expected to be a perfect fit for the show's quirky, dark world.

Source: The Hollywood Reporter

From the Creators

Writers Promise a "More Horror-Focused" Season

June 19, 2025

In a recent interview, the show's creators revealed that Season 2 will lean more heavily into the horror genre. "We're dialing up the scares," said showrunner Miles Millar. "While the mystery and humor are still there, we wanted to explore the darker corners of this world. Expect more monsters, more jumps, and a genuinely terrifying new threat."

Source: Entertainment Weekly
